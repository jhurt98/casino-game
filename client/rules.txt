start: 3 cards per player, 3 cards in middle, right of dealer starts

turn:
pick up a pair, as many matching cards, 
pick a card to make pair, 
if you cant pick any cards, you have to play a card:
    play -> creates new card with sum, if you have the sum card, and its not a pair already, you can combine cards into your play
    throw -> into table

dealer deals when everyone runs out

final round = dealer doesnt have enough for another one. if it can give everyone an extra card it does


Game Mechanics and Rules (mostly rules, idk what mechanics actually refer to
tbh):
some entities:
- Deck
- Players have a hand of cards, pile of cards
- Table
- Round

at the game start: 
- deal cards to table, and player, each get 3

each turn:
- a player must "make a play" with their hand
- they can make the kinds of stacks i described "sum" or "duplicate"
  - they can only make these stacks if they have another card in their hand that
    matches the value of the stack: the summed value or the duplicate value
  - rule: they can't make a sum stack on top of an existing
    duplicate stack. they also can't make a duplicate stack on top of an
    existing sum stack.
- they can "take" a card or stack from the table with a matching card in their
  had and pile them
- if they can't do any of these, they must "toss" a card from their hand into
  the table
- in the same turn, the player can also make any number of valid "table moves"
  - if they have the match in hand, make a "duplicate" stack
  - if they have the match in hand, make a "sum" stack
  - these stacks differ from player stacks in that you can create a sum stack
    with an existing duplicate stack. so if the table has a stack [2,2,2: 2]
    a card 3, and the player has a 5 in their hand, they can stack 3 on the
    2 stack.
  - they will eventually take all of the table moves with a matching card in
    their hand. 
  - right now, ive implemented the mechanic so that when a player drags a table
    card over a potential match, they stack and take in one go, but i didn't
    consider that they can make any number of these table moves. I'm trying to
    think of a good way of expressing this mechanic or rule to the player
